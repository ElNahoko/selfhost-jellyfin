About Nahoko

A passion project: a calm, fast place to discover films, series, anime and games, without ads and without noise.

Made by one developer, for friends and family.

Nahoko is built and run on a small personal server. No company, no investors, nothing to sell.

What you can do

  • Browse more than 30,000 films and series, with ratings, cast, trailers and every episode of every season.
  • Save favorites (sign in with your email, no password needed).
  • Tell the owner what you would like to watch, and see what others asked for.
  • Can’t choose? Press the dice for a good pick.

Where the information comes from

Ratings and titles from the IMDb datasets. Posters, pictures and descriptions from TMDB, TVmaze, AniList and Steam. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. All names, posters and trademarks belong to their owners.

Install it

On a phone, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen (Android) or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone). It opens like an app.
