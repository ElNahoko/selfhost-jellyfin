Privacy

Short version: we collect nothing about you.

No tracking. No ads. No analytics.

No third-party scripts, no tracking cookies, no fingerprinting, nothing sold or shared. Browsing the catalogue needs no account and leaves nothing behind.

Only if you sign in

  • Your email address, to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is used for nothing else: no newsletter, no marketing.
  • Your favorites and what you asked for, so they are there on every device.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in. Signing out removes it.

Good to know

  • Sign-in codes are sent by email through Resend, and nothing else is shared with them.
  • Pictures are fetched by our server, so the sites they come from never see your visit.
  • Your browser may keep a few settings on your device (for example the last tab you opened). They never leave it.

Delete everything

Ask the owner and your email, favorites and requests are removed for good.
